How voting works
  • One current vote per email-verified account per brand. Change or withdraw yours at any time.
  • We show a percentage only after 20 active Naughty or Nice votes. Not sure does not count towards that minimum.
  • The percentage is Nice votes divided by all Naughty and Nice votes. Votes expire after 12 months. Vote again to renew yours.
  • Email verification checks access to an email account. It does not prove a unique person or verify their claims. No purchase is required.
  • We exclude spam, automated votes and coordinated manipulation after review. Negative votes or a sudden surge alone are not grounds for exclusion.
  • Suspected abuse? Email help@build40for40.com with the brand and approximate time. Public comments and evidence submissions are not part of this version.
Voting privacy

Signing in uses WorkOS AuthKit. WorkOS processes your email and sign-in information. Our voting database stores your WorkOS account ID, your vote, optional reasons and dates, not your email or name. Public results contain totals only.

As in Joyo, AuthKit keeps refresh tokens in this page’s local storage so sign-in survives a reload. Tokens are never shared with shop pages or the extension.

Expired votes and inactive rate-limit records are deleted during daily maintenance. Excluded accounts keep a marker to prevent bypassing moderation; moderation decisions are recorded for review. Withdrawal removes your active vote, although an exclusion marker and moderation record may remain. Contact help@build40for40.com for account-data requests.
