What leaves your browser

Shopping signals are checked on your device. On a likely shopping page, only the website hostname is sent to our Cloudflare service. Product details, page paths, prices and shopping signals are not sent.

Results are cached in this browser session for up to five minutes. Your preferences stay on this device. Turn off the setting above to stop automatic lookups.

Cloudflare receives normal connection information when your browser contacts the service, such as your network address. No account or tracking identifier is added by the extension.

Coverage is limited to reviewed websites. B Corp evidence is information about a business, not proof that a product is ethical. Naughty or nice scoring is still being agreed.
