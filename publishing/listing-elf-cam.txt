Ho ho hold on a sec, before you buy.

The Naughty List brings reviewed company evidence to your Christmas and Black Friday shopping. Elf Cam briefly lights up a likely shopping page, then settles into a corner badge. Open its case file to see the available business evidence, source links and check dates.

What's in the sack:
- An Elf Cam intro and a compact badge that stays clear of purchase controls.
- A case file with reviewed company evidence where available.
- Settings to choose a corner or turn automatic checks off.
- Page dismissal and a retry when a lookup fails.

We're starting small, with limited, reviewed website mappings. H&M is a reviewed business match, but its current B Corp certification is unknown. Other shops may also show unknown. A retailer or marketplace does not identify the maker of every product it sells.

Naughty-or-nice scoring is still being agreed. This release does not compute an ethical rating. B Corp certification concerns a company, not an individual product. Missing evidence never means naughty.

Shopping detection happens locally in your browser. On a likely shopping page, only the hostname is sent to our Cloudflare service to retrieve stored evidence. Product text, prices, page paths and HTML stay in your browser. Settings stay on this device. No account is needed.
