How it works

Elf Cam keeps an eye on the company behind the gift.

  1. Shop like normal on H&M's website.
  2. Once a day, Elf Cam goes live for a few seconds, then shrinks to a badge in the corner.
  3. Click the badge for the case file: the evidence, where it came from and when our elves checked.

No naughty or nice verdicts yet: Santa hasn't agreed his rules. Not being on the list never means naughty. More shops and brands are on the way.

Install it

About a minute. Less than wrapping a bike.

Chrome

Until we're in the Chrome Web Store, it's a little DIY:

  1. Download naughty-list.zip [link] and unzip it.
  2. Paste chrome://extensions into the address bar.
  3. Switch on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  5. Click the puzzle piece in the toolbar and pin The Naughty List.

Works in Edge, Brave and Arc too (in Edge it's edge://extensions). Chrome may remind you a developer extension is on. That's expected until the store version arrives.

Firefox

  1. Add to Firefox [signed .xpi link].
  2. Firefox asks if you want to add The Naughty List. Click Add.

No Safari yet. It's on our list.

One-click install is on its way. We've sent it to the Chrome Web Store and the elves are reviewing the footage. Installed it by hand? You can switch when it's live.

Questions

Case notes from the North Pole.

How do you decide who's nice?
We don't, yet. Right now Elf Cam shows evidence, not verdicts: whether the company is a Certified B Corp, checked by hand against the B Lab directory, with the date. Naughty-or-nice verdicts wait until Santa's rules are agreed. It's about the company, not any single jumper.
Which brands are on the list?
H&M to start, checked by hand. We're adding more before Black Friday. If we haven't vouched for a brand, Elf Cam says "unidentified", which never means naughty.
Is Elf Cam watching me?
No. It watches brands, not shoppers. It only runs on the shops on our list. To look up the company, it sends that shop's web address (like www2.hm.com) to our server, and nothing else: no page content, no browsing history, no account.
