How it works

It's elf-explanatory.

  1. Shop like normal on H&M's website.
  2. Open any product page. The Naughty List pops up.
  3. See the reviewed business match and available evidence. H&M’s B Corp certification is currently unknown.

Just one brand for now (even Santa started with one sleigh). More shops and brands are coming down the chimney, then full naughty-or-nice ratings for staff, communities and the planet.

Unwrap it

Takes about a minute. Less than wrapping a bike.

Chrome

Until we're in the Chrome Web Store, it's a little bit of DIY:

  1. Download naughty-list.zip [link] and unzip it.
  2. Paste chrome://extensions into the address bar.
  3. Switch on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  5. Click the puzzle piece in the toolbar and pin The Naughty List.

Works in Edge, Brave and Arc too (in Edge it's edge://extensions). Chrome might remind you a developer extension is on. That's fine, it's on the nice list.

Firefox

  1. Add to Firefox [signed .xpi link].
  2. Firefox asks if you want to add The Naughty List. Click Add.
  3. That's it. Ho ho ho.

Questions

Answered by our most senior elf.

How do you decide who's nice?
Right now we show reviewed business information and B Corp evidence where available. H&M’s certification remains unknown; we do not infer a verdict from missing evidence. B Corps are independently assessed on how they treat workers, communities and the environment. It's about the company, not any single jumper.
Which brands are on the list?
Just H&M for now, checked by hand. We're adding more before Black Friday. If we haven't checked a brand, we'll say "not on our list yet", which never means naughty.
Are you watching me shop?
Shopping signals are checked locally. On a likely shopping page, only the hostname is sent to our Cloudflare API. Product text, prices and page paths stay in your browser. Cloudflare processes connection details to deliver and protect the service. No account is required. Privacy notice.
